LICENSING -- READ BEFORE ANY DISTRIBUTABLE BUILD
------------------------------------------------
Each generation file carries two independent set sources, on different terms
(upstream README.md, vendored here):

  "stats" : generated from Pokemon Showdown ladder usage. MIT licensed.
  "dex"   : transcribed from Smogon.com's written analyses. COPYRIGHT Smogon.com and
            its contributors; upstream states you "must first receive permission from
            Smogon.com before using sets from this data source in your application."

The generator reads BOTH and prefers `dex`, because dex sets are hand-built by
analysis writers and read as deliberate team-building in a way ladder aggregates do
not. That choice was made explicitly for a PRIVATE mod, where nothing is distributed.

tools/smogon_corpus.py therefore keeps a `--stats-only` switch that drops the `dex`
half of the corpus. Any build of Realidea that leaves this machine must use it, or
must carry Smogon's permission. This is a licensing constraint, not a quality one --
stats-only output is measurably weaker, and that is the trade being made: mean
absolute deviation from the eBST curve goes from 1.7 BST to 17.9, and every mega
disappears from all nine boss teams, because mega sets live almost entirely in the
`dex` half.

What the generator reads
------------------------
moves, item, ability, nature, evs. Sets are filtered against Realidea's own PBS on
the way in: a move the species cannot learn at the capped level is dropped, and any
Z-crystal item is dropped outright because Realidea's engine has no Z-move support.

Gen 8 and gen 9 are included only for their National Dex / Ubers / Anything Goes
formats, which cover gen-7-legal mons that gen-7 tiers do not reach. Their own-gen
formats are skipped -- they assume mechanics (Dynamax, Tera) this engine lacks.

Vendored rather than fetched at build time so team generation stays offline and
reproducible, matching the rest of the study.
