OU uses the 1825 cutoff (its highest published band); the other five tiers publish
1760 as their top band. Higher cutoffs mean stronger ladder players, so the teammate
correlations reflect considered team-building rather than ladder noise.

Gen 7 only, and deliberately: Realidea V4.1 is a gen-7-era Essentials v16 game, so
gen-8/9 correlations would reference mons, moves and mechanics the engine does not
have. The 2019-06 snapshot post-dates USUM's final tier shifts.

What the generator actually reads
--------------------------------
Only the "Teammates" block of each species entry, used to score off-theme picks
(TEAM-DESIGN.md §6.5). The parser also reads Abilities/Items/Spreads/Moves and
"Checks and Counters" so the whole file round-trips, but nothing downstream keys on
them yet.

"Checks and Counters" is sparse on purpose: Smogon only publishes that block above a
usage threshold, so all of gen7ou carries just 75 counter lines. That is upstream
behaviour, not a parse failure.

Licence: Smogon publishes these usage statistics openly; they are generated from
ladder play on the official Pokemon Showdown server rather than authored analysis
prose, and carry no separate copyright notice (unlike the `dex` sets in
extracted/smogon-sets/, which do -- see that manifest).

Vendored rather than fetched at build time so team generation stays offline and
reproducible, matching the rest of the study.
