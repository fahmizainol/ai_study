Smogon sample teams — source data for the tier suite (tools/make_tier_teams.py).

Source   : https://data.pkmn.cc/teams/FORMAT.json
Docs     : https://pkmn.github.io/smogon/data/teams/
Fetched  : 2026-09-06
Format   : Pokemon Showdown JSON set data, canonicalized and condensed
           (https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown/blob/master/sim/TEAMS.md#json-format)

Condensed means anything Showdown fills in by default is omitted: level 100, 31 IVs,
0 EVs. tools/reborn_names.py applies those defaults on the way in.

Teams carry optional name/author fields whose accuracy depends on the sample-team
maintainers and is not guaranteed -- gen8ou team [3] has a smogtours URL as its
"author". Treated as metadata only; nothing downstream keys on them.

Vendored rather than fetched at build time so team generation stays offline and
reproducible, matching the rest of the study's validation.

  gen7ou.json    26 teams    gen8uu.json    24 teams
  gen8ou.json    11 teams    gen6ou.json    14 teams

Only these four tiers are vendored: they sit closest to Reborn's engine generation.
Gen 1-4 teams import cleanly but are mechanically meaningless in a gen-7-era engine
(physical/special split, Dark and Steel types); gen 9 loses Tera and its newest mons.
