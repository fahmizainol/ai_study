Pokémon Run & Bun — trainer documentation manifest
===================================================
Recorded 2026-09-22.

Run & Bun is a GBA Emerald hack. Nothing here was extracted from a ROM: the source is
the game's own public documentation, shared as a Google Drive folder.

  Drive folder   https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1M-PdZrACBkGPpceTanCq_ltbGNT24lR8
  Trainer sheet  https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Ezdd8k-pDlVocd0NHUkGwZXcegfG-KTOraM_uZUsr6s
                 (exported per tab with /export?format=csv&gid=<gid>)

runandbun/
  00-Route-102.csv ... 09-Elite-Four-and-Champion.csv
      The ten sheet tabs, renamed by their first area and numbered in STORY order. The
      order is not stated anywhere in the sheet; it was read off the boss level caps in

  Mechanic Changes.txt
      Verbatim from the Drive folder. The facts the study leans on: EVs are removed,
      Gen 8 mechanics otherwise, the boss list with level caps, permanent overworld
      weather/effects per area, no bag in trainer battles.

Also in the Drive folder, not copied: "Learnset, Evolution Methods and Abilities.txt"
(no base stats -- the study uses Showdown's gen 9 numbers), runandbun.lua, and sheets for
item/Pokémon locations, move changes, post-KO switch AI and the Hall of Fame.

Every opponent Pokémon runs 31 IVs -- stated by the study's author, not by these files.
The absence of EVs is in "Mechanic Changes.txt" ("EVs are removed from the game").
